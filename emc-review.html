Power: +12V Eurorack → PTC → SS34 → TPS54202 buck (~550 kHz) → +3V3; USB-C VBUS → PTC → AMS1117-3.3 → same +3V3 rail. BLOCKER Fix before ordering

B1 ESP32 decoupling is ~20 mm from the power pin DC-002

C11 (100 nF) sits at (142.97, 71.75); U4 pin 2 (3V3) computes to (134.76, 50.66) from the raw footprint — ≈21 mm. C10 (22 µF) ≈20 mm. WiFi TX bursts pull fast sub-µs current edges [~300 mA-class, inference — no datasheet on disk]; that transient loops through ~40 mm of trace instead of a cap. Expect VDD ripple, TX spurs, possible brownout resets.

Fix: move C11 within 2–3 mm of pin 2 (space along the module's left edge is free), C10 next to it, one 3V3 via + one GND via at the cap. Espressif guideline: 0.1 µF + 10 µF at the 3V3 pin.

B2 Switching node routed like a signal SW-003 · RP-001

Net-(U1-SW) runs 19.5 mm at 0.3 mm width with 2 layer transitions down to B.Cu (vias at 146.5,120.0 and 147.25,113.5). The SW node carries the full 550 kHz di/dt and is your #1 radiator. The input hot loop (C1→U1→L1) is 26 mm² — C1 is 6.9 mm, L1 9.5 mm from U1 — putting 117 harmonics into the 30–88 MHz FM band (SW-001).

Fix: keep SW entirely on F.Cu between U1 pin 2 and L1, target <8 mm total, widen to ≥0.8–1 mm. Pull C1 adjacent to the VIN/GND pins so the hot-loop triangle tightens — every halving of loop area is −6 dB.

B3 Buck output is 3.46 V, not 3.3 V

R1=100k / R2=21k with Vref=0.6 V → 3.457 V (divider math verified; vref_source=lookup). With ±1% parts you can see ~3.53 V — above the 3.465 V (+5%) ceiling anything on the "+3V3" J3 carrier pin expects, and it eats your entire ripple margin against the ESP32's 3.6 V abs max.

Fix: R2 = 22k → 3.327 V — almost certainly what was intended. One component, big margin win. Note: this changes the buck-vs-LDO priority (see Medium M4).

B4 Two USB-C GND pads have no copper connection islands 0 & 1

The connectivity graph splits GND into 3 islands: everything on island 2, J2:A1B12 alone on island 0, J2:B1A12 alone on island 1. Those are the SMD shell tabs — assigned to GND in the schematic but sitting on F.Cu with no track, pour, or via reaching them (the In1 zone can't touch an F.Cu-only pad). Shield is grounded only through the 4 THT pins. Also: U3 (USBLC6) has only 1 GND via within 3 mm (ES-002).

Fix: one GND via at each shell tab; add a second via directly at U3 pin 2 to halve the ESD clamp-path inductance.

HIGH High priority

H1 USB pair return path RP-001 · NR-001

8 layer-transition vias on the pair; 7 of 8 have no GND via within 4.3–9.6 mm (worst 9.55 mm at (144.0, 133.5)). The pair runs 77 mm on B.Cu, whose reference is the In2 +3V3 pour at 60.5% fill — return current detours around pour voids and crosses to GND through decoupling caps. Full-Speed will still enumerate, but this is a loop antenna along the board spine.

Fix: GND via within 1 mm of every signal transition via; structural fix in H3.

H2 Feedback network far from the buck

R1/R2 sit 15–18 mm from U1 and the FB net wanders 31 mm, mostly on B.Cu, through 3 vias — a high-impedance sense line in the same neighborhood as the SW node and L1's field. Ripple-into-FB → jitter and audible output ripple.

Fix: move R1/R2/C6 to within ~3 mm of the FB pin; keep the FB trace short and direct; sense +3V3 at the load side of the output caps.

H3 B.Cu signals reference a power pour; GND plane is shredded GP-001

In1 GND fills only 65.8% and comes back as 5 disjoint filled polygons — carved by the 28 THT holes of J1/J3/J4 plus clearances in a 20.6 mm-wide strip. The GP-001 error on /−12V (71% reference coverage over 28.9 mm) is a symptom.

Fix (cleanest): make In2 GND as well (S-G-P-S) and run +3V3 as the F.Cu trace network it mostly already is. Less invasive: pour GND on B.Cu beneath the USB run and headers. Also enable a GND pour on F.Cu (antenna keepout already excludes the top end) — it shortens every cap's GND leg. 22 GND vias total is thin for 90 mm of board; add a ~5 mm stitching grid plus the transition-adjacent vias from H1.

H4 No input filtering on the +12V rail IO-001

Buck input is D1 → C1/C2 and nothing else; 550 kHz harmonics conduct straight back onto the busboard you share with audio modules — the classic "module whine in the rack" failure.

Fix: small input filter after D1 — 1 µH choke or a ≥1 A ferrite bead, flanked by 10 µF + 1 µF. Keep it before C1 so C1 stays the hot-loop cap.

MEDIUM Medium / polish

IDFinding & fix
M1USB pair hygiene: 88.8 vs 82.5 mm — 6.3 mm intra-pair skew, loosely coupled, 1.2–1.8 mm from the board edge for its whole length. FS tolerates the skew electrically, but tighten the pair (gap ≤1 mm), match within ~2–3 mm, pull ≥3 mm (5H) from the edge, ideally single-layer.
M2AMS1117 thermal (TS-002/TS-004): est. Tj 115 °C at 1.5 W, no thermal path. The SOT-223 tab is pin 2 = VOUT, so thermal vias from the tab down to the In2 +3V3 pour give thermal + electrical relief in one move. Adjacent MLCCs (C7/C8 at 6–8 mm) also run hot (TP-001s).
M3U1 EN is NO_CONNECT. TPS54202 EN has an internal pull-up so floating = enabled [inference — verify in datasheet; none synced], but a 100k from VIN to EN gives defined startup/UVLO for one resistor.
M4Power-path note: buck (3.46 V) and AMS1117 (3.3 V) both drive +3V3 with no ORing. Works by accident — the LDO can only source, so the higher rail wins and the LDO idles. After B3 the rails sit within ~30 mV of each other and will fight in the dropout region: either document "USB = bench power only, disconnect rack" or add proper ORing (ideal-diode or Schottky).
M5Carrier headers (IO-002): J3/J4 are signal-heavy with few GND pins. If GPIOs leave the board on jumper wires, each is an antenna. Interleave more GND pins; consider 22–100 Ω series resistors on edges that leave the board.
M6PDN anti-resonance (PD-001, analytical): +3V3 ~0.21 Ω at 331 MHz vs ~0.1–0.17 Ω target — a 330 pF MLCC near the buck/LDO fills the notch. Analytical estimate; B1's cap moves address the region that matters for WiFi bursts anyway.
